## Information we process

If you send an enquiry or make a direct booking, we may process your name, phone number, email address, requested dates, number and composition of guests, information about pets, special requests and our correspondence with you.

For confirmed bookings, we may also process information that accommodation providers must collect for guest registration, accounting or other legal requirements.

The website and its service providers may also process limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device details, the date and time of a request, and logs needed to keep the site secure and working.

## Why we process personal data

We use personal data when needed to:

- reply to an enquiry and take steps you request before entering into a contract;
- manage and fulfil a confirmed booking;
- communicate with you before, during and after your stay;
- meet legal requirements for guest registration, tourism, accounting, tax or other applicable matters;
- protect the website, prevent abuse, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- carry out an activity for which consent is the appropriate legal basis.

Information submitted for booking purposes is not used for marketing communications without an appropriate lawful basis.

## Legal bases

The legal basis depends on the activity. We may rely on:

- steps taken at your request before entering into a contract;
- performance of a contract;
- a legal obligation;
- our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, communicating with guests and protecting legal rights;
- your consent, where the activity is based on it.

## Recipients and service providers

We share personal data only when needed to provide a service or meet a legal obligation.

The website uses or may use the following providers:

**Cloudflare** for infrastructure, website protection and Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile is used to distinguish genuine users from automated requests and may process technical browser and device signals.

**Resend** for sending and delivering emails relating to enquiries and bookings. The content of relevant messages and information required to deliver them may be processed by Resend.

Where a booking is made through Booking.com, Airbnb or another platform, that platform also processes personal data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Information may also be disclosed to competent public authorities where required by law.

Some technology providers may process information outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

## Retention

We keep enquiries that do not lead to a booking only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related messages. As a rule, we delete them within 12 months of the last contact unless we have a justified reason to keep them longer.

We keep booking information for as long as needed to provide the stay, resolve potential disputes and meet legal record-keeping requirements for tourism, accounting, tax or other mandatory records.

We keep technical information according to our security needs and the retention rules of the relevant service providers.

When we no longer have a legal or operational reason to keep information, we delete or anonymise it.
