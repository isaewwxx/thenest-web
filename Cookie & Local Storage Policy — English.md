## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can store in your browser.

Other browser storage, including local storage and session storage, can hold settings or technical information without using a cookie.

## Our approach

We use neither advertising cookies nor website information for behavioural advertising. The website currently has no separate marketing or advertising tracking system.

## Consent choices

The cookie settings panel offers three choices: **Accept all** allows the embedded Google Maps view to load; **Necessary only** and **Reject all** keep that optional embed disabled. Your choice is saved in browser local storage so the panel does not reappear on each visit. You can reopen it from **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## Necessary technologies

The site uses limited technical storage where needed to secure and operate its forms. Where a technology is strictly necessary to provide a service you request, we may use it without prior consent to the extent allowed by law.

## Cloudflare Turnstile

The enquiry form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect against automated requests, spam and abuse.

Turnstile may process technical signals and use browser mechanisms necessary to perform its security check.

Cloudflare states that Turnstile does not access the content entered by users into form fields.

## Google Maps

The location page offers an embedded Google Maps map. It remains unloaded unless you choose **Accept all**; after consent it loads lazily as it approaches the viewport. When loaded, your browser connects to Google's servers. Google may process technical information and use its own cookies or similar technologies under its policies. You can use the rest of the website without loading the map or open Google Maps through the external link.

## Enquiry confirmation

After you submit an enquiry, the booking flow uses either session storage or a temporary cookie to verify the submission and show its confirmation. The cookie can remain for up to five minutes.

## External websites

When you open an external link to Booking.com, Airbnb, Facebook or another service, that site may use its own cookies and related technologies.

We do not control tracking technologies used by external websites.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

Blocking technologies needed for the site to work may affect features such as enquiry-form protection or confirmation.

If we add analytics, advertising or other technology that requires prior consent, we will not activate it until we have obtained that consent. We will also update this policy.

We may update this policy when the website's technologies or services change.
