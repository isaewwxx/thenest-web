## 3. Detailed Cross-Source Conflict Ledger

### Conflict 1: Seasonal Rate Brackets & Date Thresholds (Pochivka.bg vs Bizneskarta.net)

- **Pochivka.bg published rates**:
  - Low Season: `01.06 – 14.06` and `14.09 – 30.10` → **97.79 BGN (€50.00)** per room/night
  - High Season: `15.06 – 13.09` → **132.99 BGN (€68.00)** per room/night
  - Early Booking Promo: `15.05 – 14.06` (Stay 5+ nights, get 1 night free)
- **Bizneskarta.net published rates**:
  - Low Season: `15.05 – 24.06` and `06.09 – 30.10` → **100.00 BGN** per room/night
  - High Season: `25.06 – 05.09` → **130.00 BGN** per room/night
  - Early Booking Promo: `15.05 – 24.06` (Stay 5+ nights, get 1 night free)
- **Preserved Resolution**: Rates are treated as provisional and omitted from binding website checkout terms until the owners provide the canonical 2026 pricing schedule.

### Conflict 2: OTA Automated Property Classifications vs Physical Reality

- **Booking.com listing automated text**:
  - Listing 1 categorizes accommodation as a "Guest house" and automatically injects boilerplate claiming a _"fully equipped kitchenette with a dishwasher and oven"_ and _"washing machine"_.
  - Listing 2 categorizes accommodation as a "Holiday home" and claims _"free bikes"_.
- **Owner-written descriptions and photographic evidence**:
  - Photos and Airbnb owner text confirm that guest rooms feature an in-room kitchenette with refrigerator/freezer, kettle, toaster, sink, and utensils, but **no dishwasher or washing machine in the room**.
  - The cooking stove, baking oven, microwave, and BBQ grills are located in the **shared covered pavilion (навес)** in the garden, not inside individual rooms.
  - Bicycles are not verified as included equipment.
- **Preserved Resolution**: Owner-written descriptions and photographic evidence are given precedence over automated OTA category templates.

### Conflict 3: Dual-Listing OTA Architecture (Listing 1 vs Listing 2)

- Booking.com lists both `The Nest 1` and `The Nest Lozenets 2`.
- Airbnb lists both `The Nest Lozenets 1` (`1518207915595474183`) and `The Nest Lozenets 2` (`1528123016093480745`).
- Neither platform clarifies whether "1" and "2" map to physical Cabins 1 and 2, or simply represent separate OTA channel inventory allocations for calendar management.
- **Preserved Resolution**: The website models the physical property as a unified complex of 3 cabins with 6 rooms, and requests owner guidance on which OTA URLs should be linked.
